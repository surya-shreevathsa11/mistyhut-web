Our story

A Stay Rooted in Coorg

In Hulithala, about 15 kilometers from Madikeri, Misty Hut sits within a quiet coffee estate where the pace of the day naturally slows down. The surroundings stay undisturbed, and as evening approaches, a soft mist often settles across the landscape, giving the space a calm and grounded feel.

With just four rooms across two levels, the stay remains private and easy to settle into. It suits couples, families, and small groups looking for a peaceful homestay in Coorg that does not feel crowded or overdone. Thoughtful comforts like good wifi, on request meals with both veg and non veg options, and parking within the property make the experience simple, relaxed, and complete without taking away from the natural setting.

How To Find Us

Directions

How to Reach

Misty Hut is located in Hulithala, a quiet village about 15 kilometers from Madikeri, surrounded by coffee estates and natural greenery. The property is accessible by road and offers a smooth drive through some of the most scenic parts of Coorg.

From Bangalore, the journey takes around 5 to 6 hours, covering roughly 260 kilometers. Once you reach Madikeri, the route becomes quieter and more relaxed as you move closer to the estate.

After your booking is confirmed, the exact location and step by step directions will be shared to ensure a simple and comfortable arrival.
